How to read the card

  • All-in $ Energy + time + session + idle from your paste. Nothing invented.
  • Effective $/kWh All-in ÷ kWh delivered. The number that makes short L2 sessions and idle shocks comparable.
  • Fee stack Proportional bars for energy · per-min time · flat session · idle. Empty segments stay zero — honest empty state.
  • vs typical DCFC Optional callout only when you paste a reference $/kWh — never a hardcoded “live network rate.”
  • Tip footer Calm “check app before plug” — literacy, not a lawsuit pitch.
